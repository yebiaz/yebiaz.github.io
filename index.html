About me

I'm Emily Biaz, a Computation and Design / Computer Science student at Duke Kunshan University, on the dual-diploma programme with Duke University.

I've gravitated toward both code and design for as long as I've been making things, and I've stopped treating that as a choice I have to make. The work I like best sits where the two meet: a model that needs an interface, an interface that needs a model underneath it.

I'm not intimidated by new places, new people, or new subjects. I've moved between countries, languages, and disciplines enough to know I get my footing quickly.
